ORDER OF THE GAME WE SHOULD WORK ON IT
Start with MazeGenerator.java, since the maze is the foundation of your game.

Then work in this order:
1. MazeGenerator — generates the maze using DFS.
2. Tile and Map — store walls, paths, and which paths are painted.
3. Renderer — draws the maze.
4. Game and GameRunner — open the window and display it.
5. Player and InputHandler — let you move around and paint paths.
6. Enemy, CollisionDetector, and ScoreManager — add enemies, collisions, scoring, and lives.
Your first goal should be running GameRunner and seeing a generated maze on the screen.
